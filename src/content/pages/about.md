## Who we are

**AMS Builds** is a web and app development agency based in **Gujrat, Punjab, Pakistan**. We help local shops, schools, clinics, traders and companies get online with custom business websites, Android apps and software solutions that are fast, affordable and built to rank on Google.

We bring a practical, on-the-ground mindset to every digital project: clear pricing, honest timelines and support you can reach on WhatsApp or phone — no middlemen, no jargon.

## What we believe

- **Client first:** We understand how businesses in Pakistan actually operate — cash sales, WhatsApp orders, walk-in customers and word of mouth.
- **Fast & simple:** We build with Astro and modern tools so your website loads in under a second, even on 4G in Pakistan.
- **SEO & AdSense ready:** Clean semantic HTML, proper headings, meta tags and sitemaps — so Google can approve and rank you.
- **Support in your language:** Urdu and English support, Mon–Sat, 9 AM – 6 PM.

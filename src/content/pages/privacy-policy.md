## Introduction
AMS Builds ("we", "us") respects your privacy. This policy explains what information we collect when you visit ams-builds.online and how we use it.

## Information we collect
- **Contact details** you share via email, WhatsApp or contact forms (name, phone, email, project details).
- **Usage data** such as pages visited, browser type and device — collected via standard server logs and, if enabled, Google Analytics.
- **Cookies** — we use minimal cookies for site functionality. Third-party services (e.g. Google AdSense, if enabled) may set their own cookies.

## How we use information
- To respond to enquiries and provide quotes/services.
- To improve our website and content.
- To comply with legal obligations.

## Google AdSense & third parties
If Google AdSense is enabled, Google and its partners may use cookies to serve personalized ads based on your visits. You can opt out via Google Ads Settings. We do not sell personal data.

## Data retention & security
We keep contact data only as long as needed to provide services. We use HTTPS and reasonable security measures.

## Your rights
You may request access, correction or deletion of your personal data by emailing hello@ams-builds.online.

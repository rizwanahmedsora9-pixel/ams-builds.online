"How much does an Android app cost in Pakistan?" — it is the most common question we get at AMS Builds. The honest answer: **it depends on features**, but in this guide we give you real 2026 PKR ranges so you can budget with confidence.

## Android app cost in Pakistan (2026)

| App type | Cost (PKR) | Timeline |
|---|---|---|
| Simple business app (catalog, contact, maps) | 60,000 – 120,000 | 3–5 weeks |
| E-commerce / ordering app | 150,000 – 350,000 | 6–10 weeks |
| Custom app with admin panel & payments | 250,000 – 600,000+ | 10–16 weeks |
| App maintenance (monthly) | 10,000 – 30,000/month | Ongoing |

## What drives the cost?

### Features
Login, payments, push notifications, chat, maps, admin panel — each feature adds design and development time. Start with an MVP (minimum viable product) and add features later.

### Design
Custom UI/UX design costs more than a basic layout, but good design directly increases downloads and ratings on the Play Store.

### Backend & admin panel
If you need to manage products/orders from a dashboard, that is extra work — but essential for real businesses.

### Play Store publishing
Google Play charges a one-time **$25** developer fee. Professional agencies include submission and ASO (keywords, screenshots) in their package.

## Flutter vs Native: which is cheaper?

**Flutter** (one codebase for Android + iOS) is usually **30–40% cheaper** than building separate native apps. For most Pakistani businesses, Flutter is the smart choice — we use it for most client apps.

## How to save money without cutting quality

1. **Start with an MVP** — launch fast, learn from users, then expand.
2. **Reuse your website data** — if you have a website, your app can share the same backend.
3. **Provide content early** — logos, photos, product lists ready = faster delivery.
4. **Choose a local agency** — PKR pricing and direct WhatsApp support beat dollar-rate freelancers.

## Questions to ask any app developer

- What technologies will you use? (Flutter / Kotlin?)
- Will I own the source code and Play Store account?
- What is included: design, development, testing, publishing?
- What is the maintenance cost after launch?

Ranking on Google is not magic — it is a system. For local businesses in **Gujrat, Punjab and across Pakistan**, these 10 practical tips will help you appear on Google Search and Maps and get more calls, WhatsApp messages and walk-ins.

## 1. Claim your Google Business Profile
Go to google.com/business, create your profile, verify your address in Gujrat, add photos, hours and WhatsApp number. This is **free** and the single biggest local ranking factor.

## 2. Use your city in your website
Your homepage title should say what you do + where: e.g. "Furniture Shop in Gujrat" — not just "Welcome to our shop".

## 3. Get a fast website
Google ranks fast sites higher. Astro static sites (like ours) load in under 1 second — a huge advantage over slow WordPress sites.

## 4. Be mobile-first
Most Pakistani customers search on phones. Your site must look perfect on mobile, with tap-to-call and WhatsApp buttons.

## 5. Add reviews — and reply to them
Ask happy customers to leave Google reviews. Reply to every review. Businesses with 20+ recent reviews dominate local search.

## 6. Post photos regularly
Add photos of your shop, products and team to your Google profile weekly. Fresh photos = higher ranking.

## 7. Write a blog
Articles like "best clinic in Gujrat" or "school admissions 2026" bring free Google traffic — and can earn AdSense income. Our blog does exactly this.

## 8. Use proper headings and meta tags
Every page needs a clear H1, meta description and title. Our templates include SEO tags automatically.

## 9. Get listed in local directories
List your business on Pakistani directories and maps with the same name, address and phone number everywhere (NAP consistency).

## 10. Be patient and consistent
Local SEO takes 1–3 months to show results. Post, review and update regularly — consistency beats shortcuts.

## 1. Customers search before they buy

Over 80% of Pakistani consumers search online before visiting a shop. Without a website and Google presence, you are invisible to them — no matter how good your shop is.

## 2. WhatsApp + website = more sales

A website with a **WhatsApp order button** turns visitors into enquiries 24/7 — even when your shop is closed. Many of our Gujrat clients see their first online orders within weeks.

## 3. Trust and professionalism

A clean, fast website signals that your business is serious. Parents trust schools with websites. Patients trust clinics with websites. It is the digital version of a clean, well-kept shop.

## 4. Google Maps and local SEO

With a website + Google Business Profile, you appear on **Google Maps** when people search near Gujrat or Gujrat. This is free advertising that works while you sleep.

## 5. It costs less than you think

A professional business website starts at **PKR 25,000** — less than one month's shop rent in many areas. One extra customer per week covers the cost.

## What local businesses need (minimum)

- Home, About, Services/Products, Contact pages
- Google Maps + WhatsApp button
- Mobile-friendly design (most customers use phones)
- Fast loading (under 2 seconds)
- Google Business Profile linked

If you run a business in Pakistan — a shop in Gujrat, a clinic in Lahore, or a school anywhere in Punjab — you have probably asked: **how much does a business website actually cost in 2026?** Prices online range from PKR 5,000 to PKR 500,000, which is confusing. In this guide, we break down real 2026 pricing in Pakistan, what is included at each level, and how to avoid overpaying.

## Quick answer: website cost in Pakistan (2026)

| Website type | Typical cost (PKR) | Best for |
|---|---|---|
| Basic / one-page website | 15,000 – 30,000 | Small shops, portfolios |
| Business website (5–10 pages) | 25,000 – 60,000 | Shops, schools, clinics, companies |
| E-commerce website | 60,000 – 200,000 | Online stores with payments |
| Custom web application | 150,000+ | Software, dashboards, portals |

These are **one-time development costs**. You will also pay small yearly costs for domain and hosting (see below).

## What affects the price?

### 1. Number of pages and features
A 5-page business website costs far less than a 50-page portal. Features like online booking, payment gateways, WhatsApp ordering and admin panels add development time — and cost.

### 2. Design: template vs custom
Cheap templates (WordPress themes) are faster but look generic and load slowly. **Custom Astro websites** — like the ones we build at AMS Builds — are faster, more secure and rank better on Google, usually at a similar price.

### 3. Content: do you have photos and text ready?
If the developer has to write all your content and shoot photos, expect extra charges. Providing your own logo, photos and product details saves money.

### 4. SEO and Google setup
Basic meta tags are usually included. Full local SEO (Google Business Profile, keyword research, schema markup) may cost extra — but it is what brings you customers from Google.

## Hidden costs to watch out for

- **Domain (.com.pk / .com):** PKR 2,000 – 5,000 per year
- **Hosting:** PKR 5,000 – 20,000 per year (Cloudflare Pages hosting is free for static sites!)
- **Maintenance:** PKR 1,000 – 5,000 per month if you want updates done for you
- **Premium plugins/themes:** Avoid if possible — they slow your site down

> **Tip:** Ask every developer for a written breakdown: development, domain, hosting, SEO and maintenance — separately. Transparent pricing protects you.

## How to get the best value

1. **Choose a fast technology** — Astro static sites load in under 1 second and cost less to host.
2. **Get a CMS** — A built-in CMS lets you update pages, blog posts, and settings yourself without touching code.
3. **Start simple, grow later** — launch with core pages, add features as sales grow.
4. **Work with a local agency** — you get Urdu/English support and PKR pricing, not dollar rates.

## The challenge

A private academy in Gujrat with 800+ students managed attendance, fees and results on paper registers and Excel sheets. Fee tracking was chaotic, and parents had no visibility into their child's progress.

## Our solution

We built a **custom web-based school management system**:
- Student, teacher and class management
- Attendance and fee tracking with reports
- Result entry and report cards
- Automated SMS notifications to parents
- Role-based access for admin, teachers and staff

## The result

Fee collection tracking improved by **60%**, paperwork dropped by **70%**, and parents now receive instant SMS updates. The system is used daily by the entire academy.

## Technologies used
Web application, MySQL database, SMS gateway, role-based dashboard.

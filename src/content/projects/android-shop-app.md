## The challenge

A retail chain with 3 branches in Punjab wanted to move beyond phone orders. Customers had to call to check stock and prices, and staff spent hours on manual order tracking.

## Our solution

We designed and built a **Flutter Android app** featuring:
- Live product catalog with stock levels synced to their shop system
- Online ordering with cash-on-delivery and branch pickup
- Push notifications for offers and order updates
- Admin panel for managing products, orders and offers
- Full Google Play Store launch with ASO (keywords, screenshots, description)

## The result

**5,000+ downloads in 3 months**, a **4.6-star rating**, and **40% of orders now come through the app**. Staff save hours daily with automated order management.

## Technologies used
Flutter, Firebase, REST API, Google Play Console.
